# Proposal Content System

## Narrative Sequence

Use the smallest sequence that supports the recipient's decision. A strong product proposal usually follows this order:

1. **Cover / promise** — product name, audience context, one clear outcome, and proposal scope.
2. **Business value** — how the product changes the customer's or operator's current journey.
3. **How it works** — one end-to-end flow written as inputs, actions, and outputs.
4. **MVP scope** — capability groups and what each enables. Show counts only when they come from a maintained source.
5. **Experience illustration** — selected product screens or mockups connected into the core journey.
6. **Key experience** — explain the most distinctive customer-facing capability through behavior, not internals.
7. **Roadmap** — parallel workstreams, dependencies, milestones, and the point at which the full journey becomes testable.
8. **Operating model / close** — show how user groups and system surfaces connect; end by restating what the MVP proves.

Omit sections the recipient does not need. Do not add team biographies, commercial terms, technical alternatives, risk registers, or next steps unless requested.

## Writing Voice

Write for a busy business owner:

- Lead with what changes for the business or customer.
- Use short declarative sentences and concrete verbs.
- Explain one idea per paragraph.
- Name who acts: khách hàng, cửa hàng, đội ngũ vận hành, đối tác.
- Connect capabilities into a journey rather than listing disconnected software modules.
- Use measured confidence. “Giúp khách hàng chủ động khám phá” is credible; “tăng chuyển đổi 40%” is not without evidence.
- Describe technical processing from the outside: what goes in, what comes out, how quality is controlled, and who approves it.

## Reusable Copy Shapes

Use these as sentence structures, not text to copy literally:

- **Promise:** “Từ [đầu vào vận hành] đến [trải nghiệm hoặc kết quả khách hàng].”
- **Business shift:** “[Sản phẩm] giúp [người dùng] [hành động], đồng thời giúp [doanh nghiệp] [khả năng kiểm soát].”
- **Journey:** “Mỗi bước tạo ra một kết quả rõ ràng để [đầu ra cuối] đúng [tiêu chí 1], đúng [tiêu chí 2] và sẵn sàng cho [hành động].”
- **MVP framing:** “Không chỉ là [tính năng nổi bật]. Nền tảng bao gồm [chuỗi khả năng vận hành].”
- **Close:** “MVP được thiết kế để kiểm chứng toàn bộ hành trình — từ [điểm bắt đầu] đến [kết quả kinh doanh quan sát được].”

## Terminology

- Prefer one stable product name across cover, headings, diagrams, and captions.
- Avoid odd literal translations of industry terms. Keep Mobile App, Seller Portal, AR, 3D, API, and Virtual Try-On when those are the terms stakeholders use.
- Replace engineering labels with user-visible language: “quy trình tạo tài sản 3D” rather than a vendor or model name; “đang xử lý” rather than queue internals.
- Label uncertain decisions honestly: “đang nghiên cứu”, “cần kiểm chứng”, or “mở để thảo luận”. Do not place them in committed scope without that qualifier.

## Editing Pass

Before layout, read only the headings in sequence. They should tell the whole story without body text. Then remove:

- Repeated claims.
- English that adds no precision.
- Generic phrases such as “giải pháp đột phá”, “tối ưu toàn diện”, or “nâng tầm trải nghiệm”.
- Technical details the recipient cannot act on.
- Features that do not support the core journey or MVP proof.
